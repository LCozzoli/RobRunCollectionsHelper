COLLECTIONS HELPER — for ROB RUN
User guide · https://github.com/LCozzoli/RobRunCollectionsHelper

GETTING STARTED
Launch Collections Helper and keep it open while you play.
At every start the app looks for ROB RUN's Steam screenshot folder
(Steam\userdata\<account>\760\remote\4262460\screenshots) in every Steam
installation of the PC. With several Steam accounts, the folder holding the
most recent screenshot is used. A folder chosen by hand is remembered; if it
disappears, automatic search resumes.

Steam only creates that folder with your first F12 screenshot in ROB RUN. If it
does not exist yet, a "First screenshot" window explains it: take a screenshot
with F12 in game, then click "I took a screenshot". The folder is then found
by itself; otherwise you are asked to choose it (for example if you capture
with another key or another tool). "Later" closes the window; clicking the
"No folder yet" banner or Options › "Find the Steam folder automatically"
opens it again.

In game, open your bag, a car trunk or a storage (chest, nightstand…) and
press F12. When both inventories are visible, they are analyzed together.
The folder is checked every 100 ms and a complete screenshot is analyzed as
soon as it appears. The bag and trunk positions are remembered from one
screenshot to the next, so later screenshots are faster.

COLORS
Green: basic and additional collections (14 recipes).
Violet: Purple, special collections (10 recipes).
Gold: Gold, unique collections (10 recipes).
The color shows the collection, not necessarily the item's rarity.
The catalogue holds 108 distinct items and the 34 possible recipes.

DETECTION TAB
"Screenshots" banner: watched folder and watching status. Click the folder
name (or "Change") to pick another one; right-click for automatic search or
to open the folder. Click "WATCHING" to pause or resume.
Below it, a one-line summary of the last analysis.
Basic / Purple / Gold / Bag / Trunk chips: their number counts the items
found; a click hides or shows those items. "Zoom" enlarges the inventories.
On the screenshot, items worth keeping are framed; every other slot of the
inventories is dimmed. Frames follow the game's full slots (1×1, 1×2, 2×1…).
Hover a frame: its name, color and recipes, and the item lights up in its
sets. Hover an item of a set: its slots light up on the screenshot, and the
tooltip tells where it is or, if missing, where to find it. Click a frame or
an item: its recipes. Click a set number: that set in the Collections tab.
"SETS IN PROGRESS" lists every collection touched by the screenshot: found
items lit, missing ones faded, with progress ("3/4", bar, "Complete"). The
most complete sets come first. An item used by two collections is in both.
If an analysis ends while you are in Collections, the tab shows
"Detection ● N" and the taskbar icon flashes when the window is inactive.

COLLECTIONS TAB
Identical tiles (number + item icons) on two pages when "All" is selected
without a search: page 1 Basic, page 2 Purple and Gold. Switch pages with the
selector at the top, ← → or Page Up / Page Down. At 1920×1080 each page fits
without scrolling; tile size follows the window. A color filter or a search
shows every result on a single page.
  BASIC · $50,000 / collection: one row per game location (bar, gas
    station, gear, gun shop, jewelry store, pharmacy, electronics; name on
    hover), base collection (01 to 07) then its additional twin (08 to 14);
  PURPLE · $150,000 / collection: 10 special collections;
  GOLD · $500,000 / collection: 10 unique collections.
Price = reward crate value, before the Notoriety bonus (up to +25%).
"×10" marks a required quantity other than 1.
Hover an item: its name, collection and origin (where to buy it and at what
price, which furniture to search, which places have random spots, or heist
containers). ● = guaranteed, ○ = random.
Right-click an item: all its recipes, or its robrunstash page in your browser.
Search (Ctrl+F) by collection name, number or item, in any game language,
ignoring accents; ✕ or Esc clears it. Matching items are framed.
A check mark shows the items visible on your last screenshot; the "Spotted"
chip shows only those recipes.

OPTIONS (⚙)
— Reanalyze the last screenshot (F5), analyze another image (Ctrl+O).
— Change the folder, find it automatically in Steam, open it.
— Set the bag or trunk area by hand, restore automatic areas (also by
  right-clicking the screenshot).
— Watch the folder.
— Move analyzed screenshots to the Recycle Bin: also the "Auto Recycle Bin"
  toggle next to "WATCHING". On by default; one click turns it off and the
  choice is kept. Only a screenshot from the watched folder that was analyzed
  successfully is moved to the Windows Recycle Bin (recoverable), after the
  result is shown and saved. Images opened from another folder, thumbnails,
  unreadable screenshots or files changed during analysis are never touched.
— Language: "Automatic" follows the language chosen in ROB RUN, otherwise the
  one set for the game in Steam, otherwise Windows'. 13 languages: English,
  French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian (the game's six: item and place
  names as in the game), Italian, Polish, Turkish, Ukrainian, Simplified
  Chinese, Japanese, Korean (item names in English). Changing the language
  restarts the window; settings and screenshots are kept.
— Zoom on inventories; keep the window on top (game in windowed or borderless
  mode; exclusive full screen may hide it).
Window size and position are remembered.

SHORTCUTS
F1 help · F5 reanalyze · Ctrl+O analyze an image · Ctrl+F search ·
Ctrl+1 / Ctrl+2 switch tab · Esc clear the search.
Closing the window stops the program completely.

HOW INVENTORIES ARE FOUND, AND LIMITS
The bag and the trunk are found from their slots and position only, without
reading any text: the game can be in any language. An inventory grid is the
only area of the screen whose dividers repeat every 62 UI pixels both ways
(6 columns). The bag is the rightmost grid; a storage (9 rows, level with the
bag) or a car trunk (8 rows below its two tabs) is the grid on its left. For a
car, the first tab (trunk) must be active (green); with the second tab
(cargo) the grid is ignored. The pouch is excluded.
Only visible items can be recognized. The app cannot read a closed trunk and
does not keep its old content. For a scrolling storage, scroll and take a new
screenshot. Stack quantities are not read. The app does not know which
recipes are currently active at your table. A hidden, very small icon, one
covered by a tooltip or changed by a game update may be missed; uncertain
matches are discarded.
If an inventory is not found (different interface, slots heavily covered):
1. Analyze an image where this inventory is open.
2. Right-click the preview (or ⚙ Options) and set the bag or trunk area.
3. Draw a rectangle around its whole grid, six columns, locked slots
   included. Each area is remembered separately.
4. "Restore automatic areas" in the same menu clears the manual areas.

PRIVACY
Everything is analyzed on your PC. No access to the game process, no
injection, no keyboard hook (the game uses Easy Anti-Cheat), no account, no
screenshot sent and no network connection while in use. No automatic start
with Windows. Only "Open its page" (right-click) opens your browser, on
request.
Item icons and texts come from ROB RUN's own files; images belong to their
owners. Unofficial fan tool.
Community recipes and item origins: https://robrunstash.vercel.app/

FILES (next to the program)
settings.json: watched folder and preferences.
last-result.json: last analysis with names, colors and coordinates.
application.log: errors, if any.
